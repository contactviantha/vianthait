Our Story

Built with Purpose. Engineered for the Long Game.

Viantha Solutions was founded in 2026 with a clear conviction — the banking technology market needed a different kind of partner. One that combines deep financial domain knowledge with modern engineering, and serves clients with the same rigour and commitment regardless of their size or geography.

We started with a focused mandate: banking technology, AI solutions, and specialist IT talent. Our team brings expertise across core banking platforms, payment systems, regulatory compliance, and AI automation — serving financial institutions across Asia, the US, and UK.

Every decision at Viantha is shaped by a long-term vision: to build a globally trusted technology enterprise that eventually contributes to the future of digital banking.
