Solutions

Personal Loan Technology Services

Practical, India-compliant solutions for the challenges personal loan lenders face today

Digital Loan Origination (LOS)

Configurable loan origination systems covering application, verification, underwriting, sanction, and disbursement for personal loans.

  • Online application journeys and lead capture
  • Rule-based work queues and verification steps
  • Sanction letter and e-agreement workflows
  • Disbursement integration with banks and wallets

eKYC & Identity Verification

Complete digital KYC stack with Aadhaar OTP, PAN verification, CKYC registry checks, and document extraction — compliant with RBI and PMLA.

  • Aadhaar eKYC (OTP + offline XML)
  • PAN verification and DIN checks
  • CKYC registry upload and matching
  • Liveness and selfie matching for onboarding

Credit Underwriting & Scoring

Credit decision engines combining bureau data, bank statement analysis, and alternative data into transparent underwriting outcomes.

  • Bureau integration (CIBIL, Experian, Equifax, CRIF)
  • Bank statement analysis and income detection
  • Rule + score-based decision engine
  • Explainable AI credit risk models

Loan Management System (LMS)

Servicing, repayment, interest calculation, NPA tracking, and collections-ready loan management for personal loan portfolios.

  • EMI scheduling and interest computation
  • Prepayment, part-payment, and foreclosure
  • Overdue, NPA, and provisioning reporting
  • GL/accounting integration for the books

AI & Automation in Lending

Intelligent automation across the lending lifecycle — reducing processing time, cost-per-loan, and default risk.

  • Document verification and OCR extraction
  • Fraud rule engine and anomaly detection
  • Chatbot-assisted loan applications
  • Automated collection prioritisation

RegTech & Compliance for Lenders

Technology to stay compliant with RBI digital lending, fair practices, AML, and data protection requirements.

  • RBI digital lending guidelines mapping
  • AML transaction monitoring and STR automation
  • Data localisation and consent management (DPDP)
  • Audit trails and regulatory reporting
