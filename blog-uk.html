What UK Teams Actually Hand Over

Integration work travels well because it has edges: a documented API, an auth flow, webhooks and tests that pass or fail. Three categories keep arriving from UK clients.

Onboarding, KYC and affordability

Onboarding is where UK lenders lose applicants, so identity and affordability work dominates the first sprint. That means wiring document and liveness vendors, pulling bureau data, capturing consent the way a reviewer expects, and writing the audit trail your compliance officer asks for in two years. See how we scope those flows on our API integration page.

Credit, lending and decisioning

A UK credit API integration offshore engagement usually means bureau requests, affordability checks, a decisioning service and the schedules that follow. The interesting part is not the score call. It is the states around it: pending referrals, stale files, consent that expires between quote and drawdown, and a rejection reason that has to survive into the customer outcome report.

Payments and reconciliation

Payment scope covers initiation, refunds and a ledger that has to agree daily. Teams without production traffic estimate this at half of what it takes, because exception states stay undocumented until they bite.

FCA and PSD2: What the Rules Actually Require

The Financial Conduct Authority regulates UK financial services. Firms need authorisation or an appointed representative arrangement, and whichever route you take, the obligations touching engineering are similar: records you can produce, access you can evidence, and a complaint path a customer can follow. An FCA compliance software developer is really just someone who treats those artefacts as part of the build rather than a document written the week before an assessment.

PSD2 sits underneath the payment products. It created account information service providers and payment initiation service providers, and it introduced strong customer authentication: two elements from knowledge, possession and inherence, with narrowly defined exemptions. A PSD2 API developer India team earns its fee on the edge cases rather than the happy path. Authentication that expires halfway through a redirect, exemptions that apply to one merchant and not another, refunds that arrive after the mandate changed, and webhooks that fire twice against a payment you have already reconciled.

One honest observation: guidance and the Open Banking standard are updated on a schedule you do not control, and bank vendor security reviews run for months rather than days. Build against the contract you hold today and keep the consent model flexible enough to absorb the next revision.

Open Banking As It Stands Today

UK open banking follows the standard published by the Open Banking entity and applies to the largest UK current account providers. An FCA Open Banking API integration work package covers consent, account information, payment initiation and the lifecycle that sits around all three: grant, renew, revoke, and the audit trail for each. If you are shortlisting an Open Banking API UK developer India team, ask how they modelled revocation on their last build. It gets skipped and then rediscovered during an assessment.

Neobank backends are the other large category. A UK neobank backend developer India engagement typically covers account opening, ledger events, card programme integrations, standing payments and reporting, all sitting behind services you cannot change unilaterally. Nobody sells you their core, so the job is careful integration and honest reconciliation.

On naming: nobody can hire Monzo or Starling engineers, so a search for a Monzo Starling API developer India describes a profile rather than a person. What actually matters is that the team builds on the same open banking rails used by Monzo and Starling, and can show you how they handled consent revocation or a failed payment initiation on a previous system.

Variable recurring payments deserve their own line: schedule, mandate, pause and cancel semantics, and the reporting that follows a payer who changes their mind mid-month all outlive the initial integration. Budget accordingly.

Why British Fintechs Outsource to India

UK firms have contracted Indian IT services for three decades, so a British fintech outsource India conversation rarely needs a business case built from scratch. As an Indian IT company UK projects keep returning to, we can point to banking and lending work rather than references pulled from a template: a Tamil Nadu engineering team in Trichy, a Singapore headquarters for contracts, and direct access to the people writing the code. No account managers sit between you and the code.

Hours work. India runs five and a half hours ahead of London, so core hours of 1 pm to 6:30 pm in Trichy cover 7:30 am to 1 pm in the UK during winter. Your morning standup and our afternoon delivery sit in the same window, which we write into the engagement note rather than assuming.

Data handling is the part UK buyers rightly press on. UK GDPR applies to personal data regardless of where the processing happens, and transfers need a documented legal basis with standard safeguards attached. Our processes follow ISO 27001 practices, engineers work inside your access controls, and no production personal data is used in development.
