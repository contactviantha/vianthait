Our Process

How We Fill Your Requirement

1

Requirement submission

Share the role, skills, experience, duration, and whether on-site or remote. Use our contact form or email us directly.

We send you 2–4 pre-screened CVs from our talent pool. Every profile is reviewed for skills, communication, and availability.
