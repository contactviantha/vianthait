Use Cases

Who This Is For

Real scenarios from banks, NBFCs, and fintech teams we work with.

Cooperative Bank Going Digital

Your cooperative bank runs core banking on a legacy system and needs mobile banking, internet banking, and UPI for depositors. Viantha builds the digital layer with core banking APIs and NPCI-compliant UPI integration — delivered in 8–10 weeks with full certification support.

NBFC Mobile Banking App

You are an NBFC launching a customer app for loans and deposits. Viantha builds the mobile banking app with eKYC onboarding, loan account views, payments, and in-app support — wired to your LOS/LMS and bank accounts.

Fintech Platform on Open Banking

You are building a fintech platform in Tamil Nadu and need secure access to bank data through Account Aggregator or OCEN rails. Viantha implements the AA framework and API gateway so your product can onboard and underwrite customers compliantly.
